Journey through the Bible together

A family trivia quest through 100 Bible stories. Every player answers questions made for their age, and every story you finish wins a card for your collection.

See the journeys Get help

Questions for every age

Each player picks their own level, so a four-year-old and a grandparent can play the same story with questions made for them. There are nine questions for every story at every level, 3,600 in all.

  • Little LightsAges 4–6Gentle questions with three big, friendly answers.
  • ExplorersAges 7–9Story questions for confident readers.
  • TorchbearersAges 10–12Deeper details for sharp-eyed story fans.
  • Lantern KeepersTeens & grown-upsHard questions that send grown-ups back to the passage.

Play trivia together

Family Night turns one iPhone into a game for the whole room. Two to six players take turns passing the phone, and each one answers at their own level. A scoreboard keeps count between turns, and the night ends on a winners' podium.

Win a card for every story

Answer all three questions about a story correctly to win its card. Each of the 100 cards has its own artwork and a rarity of Rare, Epic or Legendary, and every player fills their own album. A new card also earns a helper for tough questions, like the Star of Wisdom, which sweeps away two wrong answers.

Read and listen

Every story can be read aloud, retold at three reading levels or read in full from the Berean Standard Bible or the King James Version.

More journeys on the way

We're planning new journeys for holidays like Christmas and Easter, journeys through single books of the Bible and collections about the people of the Bible, from the kings of Israel to its women of faith.

Future journeys may be sold separately from the Full Quest.

Made for families

The first three journeys, 30 stories, are free with no time limit. The Full Quest is a single one-time purchase, not a subscription, that unlocks the other 70 stories for every player on your device. You can restore it on your other devices with the same Apple ID. It is not shared with other members of a Family Sharing group.

There are no ads or accounts. Nothing is collected about you or your children. Everything commercial sits behind a grown-ups check.
