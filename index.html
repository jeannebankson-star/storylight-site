Together time

One room.
Lots of little lights.

Share one screen for a family story quest, or bring 2 to 6 nearby iPhones and iPads together for trivia.

Create a room, share its six-character code and let each player choose their question level. Different Apple accounts work, too.

Keep Wi-Fi on, allow Local Network access and stay nearby. Nearby play doesn’t need an internet connection.

Room to explore.
Simple to start.

Try 10 stories for free. The optional Full Quest is a one-time purchase that unlocks the remaining 90 stories for every local player on your device. No subscription.

Restore the purchase on your other devices with the same Apple account. Progress stays on each device, and the purchase isn’t shared with other Apple accounts.
