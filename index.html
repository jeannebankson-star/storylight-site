Made for families

The first fifty stories are free. One purchase unlocks the other fifty for everyone in your Family Sharing group. There are no ads, no accounts, and nothing is collected about you or your children. Everything commercial sits behind a grown-ups check.

Read and listen

Every story can be read aloud, retold at three reading levels, or read in full from the Berean Standard Bible or the King James Version.
