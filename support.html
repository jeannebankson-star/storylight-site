Common questions

What is free and what costs money?

Start with ten free stories: the first five from In the Beginning in the Old Testament and the first five from Miracles and Parables in the New Testament. You can begin either sample journey without finishing the other. The Full Quest is a single one-time purchase that unlocks the other ninety stories in the current library. It is not a subscription. Every player on your device gets it, but it is not shared with other members of a Family Sharing group.

Where are Settings?

Tap the gear icon at the top right of Journeys, Cards or Together. Settings includes player profiles, read-aloud preferences and the For Grown-Ups section.

Can we play trivia on separate devices?

Yes. Nearby rooms connect two to six iPhones or iPads, and everyone can use their own Apple account. Open Together on the host device, choose Host a room, enter a nickname and create the room. On each other device, choose Join with a code and enter the host's six-character code. Each person chooses their own question level. The host can start once a second player joins.

Keep the devices nearby with Wi-Fi on, allow Local Network access when asked and leave Storylight open. Using the same Wi-Fi network is the simplest setup. No internet connection is needed. These rooms work nearby, not between homes over the internet.

The game uses stories available on the host's device. Joining a room does not permanently unlock purchases or cards on a guest's device. You can also use Share one screen in Together for a family story quest on one device.

How do I buy the Full Quest?

Open Settings, tap For Grown-Ups and complete the “For adults” check. If you're a minor, ask a parent or guardian to complete this step. The Full Quest is the first item. Payment goes through your Apple ID, and Apple's normal receipt and refund rules apply.

Why does my purchase say “Purchase pending”?

Your purchase needs approval or another action before it can finish. Follow any instructions from the App Store. If your family uses Ask to Buy, a parent or guardian must approve the request. The Full Quest unlocks automatically once the purchase completes, right away if the app is open or the next time it opens. Restore the purchase on another device using the Apple ID that bought it.

I bought it on one device. How do I get it on another?

Sign in with the same Apple ID that bought it, then open Settings, tap For Grown-Ups, complete the adult check and tap Restore purchases. It does not carry over to other members of a Family Sharing group. Progress does not sync between devices; only the purchase does.

We got a new phone. Where did our cards go?

Progress lives on the device and is included in your iCloud or computer backup. Restoring the new phone from that backup brings every player's cards back. If the new phone was set up fresh, the progress from the old one cannot be recovered. The purchase can always be restored.

How do I add or remove players?

Open Settings and look under Players. Tap Add a player to create a profile, or tap an existing player to edit their name, avatar or question level. To remove a player, swipe left on their row, tap Remove and complete the grown-ups check and confirmation. Removing a player deletes their saved cards and progress.

What happens to my cards when I change question level?

Each player has a separate card collection and progress for every question level. Switching levels keeps earlier cards and progress saved. Return to a level to pick up where you left off, or browse your collections in Cards.

Are more stories or physical cards coming?

More Bible stories and journeys are coming soon. Future story packs may be offered separately from the current Full Quest. Physical Storylight cards are also coming soon. Ordering is not available yet; the cards you collect in the app are digital.

Can I start over?

Yes. Open Settings, tap For Grown-Ups, complete the adult check, then tap Reset all progress. This removes every player and every won card on the device. Purchases are kept.

Read-aloud is not working.

Check that Read stories aloud is switched on in Settings. Narration plays even with the ringer switch on silent, but not if the volume is all the way down or headphones are connected elsewhere. If a story still stays quiet, force-quit the app and open it again.

Which Bible translations does the app use?

Full passages are available in the Berean Standard Bible and the King James Version. You can switch between them in Settings. The retellings at each reading level are written for the app and are not a translation.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all refunds for App Store purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase and choose the item.
