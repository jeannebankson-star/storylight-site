Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

The short version

We do not collect personal information through the Storylight Quest app. There are no app accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Profiles and progress are stored on your device. If you choose to host or join a nearby trivia room, the devices in that room exchange the nickname, question level and game activity needed to play together. This information is not sent to a Storylight server.

Who we are

Storylight Quest is published by AKS Services LLC ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@storylightquest.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, such as screenshots. The Contact support link in the app fills in the app version and iOS version for you. We use what you send only to reply to you.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following in its own private storage on the device:

  • Player profiles: the name typed in for each player, the chosen avatar, the question level and the date the player was added.
  • Progress: cards won and the date each was won, tokens earned and journey completion for each player at each question level.
  • Settings: read-aloud, haptics, sounds and Bible translation preferences.
  • Answer history: for each player and question level, which questions they have answered, how many times each was right or wrong, when each was last answered, its step in a practice schedule and, for each story, the date they had first answered all of its questions correctly at least once. This version of the app records it but does not show it yet. It is kept so a later update can show each player's progress and help them practice.
  • A record of which purchases the device is entitled to, so the app works offline.

This stored information is not sent to us. Optional nearby rooms share only the information described below with participating devices. If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup under your own Apple ID and Apple's terms. You can delete all of it at any time by deleting the app. Reset all progress (in Settings, under For Grown-Ups and behind the adult check) deletes all of it except the purchase record, which stays because purchases belong to your Apple ID.

Removing a player deletes that player's profile, progress and answer history. If an update changes how the app saves progress, or if saved progress cannot be read, the app keeps a safety copy of the earlier file in the same private storage. A copy like that can still include players who were removed. Reset all progress deletes those copies too.

A player's name can be anything you like. It does not have to be a real name.

Optional nearby family rooms

Nearby trivia is optional. When you host or join a room using its six-character code, the participating devices communicate directly over the local network. The app asks for Local Network permission to find and connect to nearby rooms. There is no Storylight server handling the game.

The room exchanges your chosen nickname, question level, connection status and game activity. Game activity includes the current questions and answer choices, submitted responses, answer explanations and scores. Each guest receives their own question and answer feedback; the room's player list and scores are shared with participants. Temporary room and player identifiers are also exchanged so devices can manage the game and reconnect.

Your Apple account, purchase records and saved card collection are not shared with other players. Room nicknames can be made up and do not need to be real names. The room has no chat or contact-sharing feature. We do not receive this nearby game information.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store using your Apple ID. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase is valid. We do not receive your name, email address, payment details or any other personal information from a purchase. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Storylight Quest is made for families to play together, so players may include children under 13. We do not collect personal information through the app, including from children. There is no app account, chat or contact-sharing feature. Optional nearby rooms share the nickname and game information described above with participating devices. The app shows no advertising. Purchases, external links and progress reset are placed behind a grown-ups check to help keep children from reaching them on their own.

If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, contact us at privacy@storylightquest.com and we will delete it.

Links to other services

The reading screen offers a link to open the current passage on bible.com (YouVersion). The link sits behind the grown-ups check. Once you leave the app, the privacy policy of that service applies. We do not receive any information about what you do there.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive your IP address under its own privacy policy when serving the site. The Montserrat typeface is hosted with the website, so loading it does not contact Google Fonts. This site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Apple's App Privacy label

The app's listing on the App Store reports Data Not Collected. This describes information collected by the developer; the optional direct exchanges between nearby players are described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Changes that affect how data is handled will also be described in the app's release notes.
